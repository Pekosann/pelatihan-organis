🔗 **Tonton di YouTube:** [Organ Lessons: Scales](https://youtu.be/JvhIBkI06I8)

This is a beginner organ lesson focused on practicing scales. The instructor walks through scale construction using the whole-step / half-step pattern (e.g., *"starting point, skip this key, hold... skipping that one — half"*), demonstrating an **A♭ scale** with both hands. He emphasizes that scales are absolutely fundamental — *if you can't play scales, you have nothing to play* — and urges students to spend even a whole afternoon on a single key, promising they'll thank themselves later.

He encourages varied practice: playing the scale up and down, in different octaves, with hands together, hands apart, and stretched as far apart on the keyboard as possible, since playing with both hands independently is essential on the organ. He closes by previewing the next clip, where pedal (foot) work will be added.
